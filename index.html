academic and artistic projects, mixed media, mysterious aims. follow a line (as above so below).

Projects

  • Capital Dreams: an ALIFE research cycle with Cesare Carissimo on Capital, Labour, AI, and whether the technological system itself might be alive. Media: publications, video, music, digital art.
  • L. A. S.: Libertas Aurea Silvarum is an ongoing citizen science project in dendrometrics. The purpose is to locate, measure, and document the largest, and oldest trees of the Tricity area in Poland and to petition the authorities to grant them the status of natural monuments. In collaboration with WG. Media: cartography, dendrometric data, photography.
  • P o w e r L i n e s: a conspiracy mapped out as lines; through them the precession of power can be traced, spied on, and followed all the way to the top. Media: digitised analog photography, rotoscoping.
  • 2xy: a visual novel designed by the now defunct unfinished games studio in collaboration with Maciej Packi. Media: C, digitised analog photography.
  • mall: a top down simulation of a liminal space experience, following the journey of a skeleton made of gold in an abandoned mall. A tribute to vaporwave aesthetics in collaboration with B.A.K. Media: C++ and C, digital pixel art, music.
