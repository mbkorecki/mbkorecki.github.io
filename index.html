Research Statement. My research investigates how artificial intelligence interacts with complex social and infrastructural systems. I combine machine learning, complex systems science, and philosophy of technology to study how algorithmic decision systems shape collective behavior, governance, and large-scale coordination. A central goal of my work is developing AI systems that remain robust, transparent, and socially aligned when deployed in real-world socio-technical environments.
